# Trend analysis

Apply the current runtime date, not example dates or hypothetical metrics in external examples.

1. Define the trend, observation period, audience and measurable signals before collecting examples.
2. Build a dated timeline from primary releases, pricing changes, published evaluations and observable customer behavior.
3. Compare like with like: consistent definitions, units, geography and periods. If a time series is unavailable, describe individual signals rather than asserting growth rates.
4. Separate attention from adoption and adoption from willingness to pay. Social engagement, search interest or GitHub stars alone do not establish a market.
5. Seek countertrends, saturation, churn, failed entrants and dependency risks. Explain alternative causes for a spike.
6. Describe plausible scenarios and leading indicators to monitor, with evidence strength and falsification criteria. Do not assign fabricated probabilities or extrapolate exponential growth from a short window.
7. Connect any business implication to an accessible buyer and a testable offer through `ai-opportunity-validation`.

Monitoring plans may be drafted, but scheduled jobs, external alerts, paid subscriptions or campaigns require specific authorization.
